Privacy policy: Easier Transcribe

Last updated: 9 November 2026.

Easier Transcribe is a website (transcribe.easiernow.co) and a Chrome extension, both made by Easier Now. This policy has one section for each. The extension section is the second one.

Questions: use the contact form.

The website: transcribe.easiernow.co

The short version

  • A video or audio file you pick is never uploaded. Speech recognition runs in your browser on your own device.
  • Your transcript stays in the page. We do not receive or save it.
  • No account, no ads, no cookies of our own, and no analytics code on the page.
  • Two things do reach a server of ours: a YouTube link you paste, and an email address you choose to type into the optional list box. Both are described below.

When you paste a YouTube link

Your browser cannot ask YouTube for captions directly, so the page asks a small function on our own site, /api/youtube. It receives the 11-character video ID and a language code. It asks YouTube for that video's captions and sends them back to your browser. Our code stores nothing and writes no log of it. The site is hosted by Cloudflare, which handles the request like any web request and may keep the returned caption text in its cache for up to 30 minutes. If a video has no captions, or YouTube refuses the request, you can transcribe the file instead and nothing is sent anywhere.

When you join the email list

The box after a result is optional. If you type an address and press the button, the page sends that address, and nothing else, to /api/subscribe. It is added to a list held by our email provider, Resend, so we can send you the setup steps and the odd tip. Your transcript, the file name and the video link are never sent. Every email has an unsubscribe link. To be removed, use it or write to us through the contact form.

Other places the page connects

  • huggingface.co: the first time you transcribe a file, your browser downloads the speech model (about 80 MB, or about 500 MB with "More accurate"). Hugging Face sees a normal file download. No audio or text is sent.
  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com): the page loads its typeface from Google, so Google sees your address like any site that does.
  • Cloudflare: hosts the site and its two functions.

What is saved on your device

The model files, in your browser's cache so they download once, and your "More accurate" choice. Clearing the site's data in your browser removes both.

The Chrome extension

The short version

  • We run no servers for this extension. We receive nothing from it.
  • No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.
  • Your transcripts are saved in your own browser. Only you can see them.
  • Speech recognition runs on your device. Your audio is not uploaded.

What the extension looks at

It looks at a page only when you click its button.

Then it reads the page for video and audio: titles, addresses, poster images, lengths, and links to YouTube videos and media files.

This stays on your device. It is used to build the list you pick from.

What is saved on your device

  • Your transcripts: the title, the address of the video, a thumbnail address or small still, the text with times, the date, and the method used. Stored in the browser's own storage for this extension (IndexedDB).
  • Your choice of the "More accurate" switch. Stored in the extension's local storage.
  • A short-lived note that passes your pick from the popup to the results tab. It is removed as soon as the tab takes it.
  • The speech model files, kept in the browser's cache so they download once.

You can delete one transcript or all of them in the results tab. Removing the extension removes everything it saved.

Where the extension connects

Only to these, and only to do what you asked:

  1. youtube.com. When you pick a YouTube video, it asks YouTube for that video's captions. This runs from the page you are on, using your own signed-in session, or from the extension for players embedded on other sites. YouTube sees an ordinary request for that video's captions. Their privacy policy applies to that.
  2. i.ytimg.com. YouTube thumbnails shown in the list.
  3. The site the video or audio is on. When you pick a file, the extension downloads it from where it already lives, with your session on that site. Poster images are loaded from the same site.
  4. huggingface.co. The first time you transcribe a file, it downloads the model files (about 80 MB, or about 500 MB with "More accurate"). Hugging Face sees a normal file download. No audio or text is sent. Their privacy policy applies.
  5. googlevideo.com. Only for a YouTube video that has no captions, and only if you allow it when Chrome asks. The extension downloads that video's audio so it can be transcribed on your device.

Nothing else. The code that runs the speech model, ONNX Runtime and ffmpeg ship inside the extension. It loads no code from the internet.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store, sell or share your data. We never receive it.
  • We do not use cookies or identifiers of our own.
  • We do not read your browsing history or watch pages you have not clicked the button on.
  • We do not use your data for ads, credit or lending.

Permissions

The reasons for each permission are in the store listing. In short: read the page you clicked on, save your transcripts, and read YouTube captions.

Children

The extension is not aimed at children and collects nothing from anyone.
