Turn any video, audio or YouTube link into text

Free. No ads. Nothing to sign up for. Whisper runs in your browser, so your files never leave your device.

You recorded a call. Now you need the words.

  1. Step 1: Drop in the file

    Drag the recording onto the box, or press it and pick the file. Video and audio both work. The file stays on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

  2. Step 2: Wait for the first run to get ready

    The first time, your browser downloads the speech model once. The default model is 80 MB. On a computer with a graphics chip there is a more accurate 500 MB option. After that it is saved in your browser, so the next file starts straight away.

  3. Step 3: Watch the text appear

    The words fill in as the recording is read. Keep the tab open until it finishes.

  4. Step 4: Copy it or save it

    Copy the text, or download it as text, text with times, SRT or VTT.

Questions people ask

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no plan to pay for and no account to make.

Is my recording uploaded?

No. Whisper runs in your browser on your own computer. We have no server that receives your file or your text.

How long does it take?

The first run includes a one-off model download. In our test, a 99 second video on a slow connection took 106 to 118 seconds including that download. Longer files take longer.

How long a file can I use?

We have tested one file, 99 seconds long. Very long recordings need a lot of memory on your computer, so a very long file may not finish. We have not set a limit.

What languages does it handle?

The Whisper models we use are English only. YouTube captions come in the language the video has.

Which formats can I save?

Text, text with times, SRT and VTT.
