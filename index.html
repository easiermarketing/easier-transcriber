Turn any video, audio or YouTube link into text

Free. No sign-up. Your files stay on your device.

On a video right now? The free Chrome extension turns it into text in one click.

How it works

  1. Add a file or paste a link Video or audio. A YouTube link works too.
  2. Wait for the text The first time takes a little longer while the speech model downloads once.
  3. Copy it or save it Text, text with times, SRT or VTT.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. No plan to pay for and no account to make.

Is my recording uploaded?

No. A file you pick is never uploaded. It is turned into text in your browser, on your own computer. Two things do reach us: if you paste a YouTube link, our page function receives the video ID and asks YouTube for the captions (we store and log nothing, though Cloudflare, who host the site, may cache the caption text for up to 30 minutes), and if you join the email list, we receive the address you type. The privacy page has the detail.

How long does it take?

The first time takes a little longer, because a one-off download of the speech model (80 MB by default) comes first. After that it loads from your browser's cache. Very long recordings need a lot of memory, so a very long file may not finish.

What can I save?

Text, text with times, SRT and VTT.

What languages does it handle?

Files are transcribed in English only. YouTube captions come in the language the video has.
